Act I · The Laws
  [x] One authority: characters only propose, and a single referee decides what really happens
  [x] Fiction with physics: every rule of the world is written down, from time and cause to bodies, money and magic
  [x] One anatomy for all: hero, wolf or baker, each has a body that hungers, ages and bleeds

Act II · Lives That Grow
  [x] Born, grow up, grow old: child → adult → elder, and death is final
  [x] Power is earned: levels, skills, class tiers and hard-won breakthroughs
  [x] Change your path: the farmer can take up the sword
  [ ] Scars that stay: injuries and losses shape who you become
  [ ] Learn from others: copy the heroes you admire, pick up habits, build a sense of self

Act III · Minds in the World
  [x] Characters who act on rumours, not truth; a tip is only as good as its source
  [x] No scripts: needs, ambitions and fears are weighed every tick, and choices stick
  [ ] Nobody is born a hero; significance has to be earned

Act IV · Leave a Mark
  [x] Places that scar: a battlefield keeps its wound after the fighting ends
  [x] Heirs inherit the estate, the heirloom and a dying wish
  [ ] Borders that shift, and factions that rise, rival and march to war
  [ ] Every system connected: one battle scars a region, and the scar drives its people out

Act V · Rise and Ruin
  [ ] Calamities that can unmake a region; decline is an ending, not a bug
  [ ] Hamlets that grow into towns, and cities that fall to ruin
  [ ] Families, feuds and fortunes carried across generations
  [ ] History that drifts into myth, and magic as real as weather

Final Act · One World, Many Games
  [ ] Discover, don't be told: you only see what the world leaves behind
  [ ] Step in as a lone adventurer, run a guild, rule a kingdom, or watch over it all like a god
